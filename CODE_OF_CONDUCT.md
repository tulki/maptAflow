# Code of Conduct

maptAflow helps people break big goals into small steps.
This project should work the same way: anyone can take a small step and help.

## Our pledge

Everyone is welcome here, whatever their age, background, experience, gender, origin, religion or ability.
We judge ideas and code, never people.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient, especially with newcomers.
- Give feedback on the work, not the person.
- Accept criticism calmly and say thanks for it.
- If you were wrong, say so and move on.

## What is not allowed

- Insults, harassment or personal attacks.
- Sexual language or imagery.
- Publishing someone's private information without permission.
- Anything that would make a reasonable person feel unsafe or unwelcome.

## Reporting

If someone breaks these rules, contact the maintainer, Tukubayev Kudaibergen ([@tulki](https://github.com/tulki)), privately.
Every report is read and kept confidential.
The maintainer may warn, temporarily ban or permanently ban anyone who breaks these rules.

## Scope

This code applies in the repository, issues, pull requests and any other project space,
and when someone officially represents the project.
